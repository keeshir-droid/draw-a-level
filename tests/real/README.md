# Real maze photos (Rishi adds these before M1)

These photos are the real test. They're gitignored and never committed.

**Please draw and photograph at least 4 mazes**, with your phone's camera, the way a normal person would (handheld, room light, not perfect):

1. **Neat:** pen on plain white paper, with clear paths.
2. **Rushed and messy:** quick and wobbly, with a few gaps in the lines and a corner that's a bit cramped.
3. **On a notebook page:** ruled or grid paper, so we can check the notebook lines get wiped.
4. **With holes:** 3–4 **filled-in dots** drawn somewhere in the paths (these become holes).

**Nice to have:**
- one in a different pen colour (blue or red)
- one drawn sideways (landscape)
- one in pencil (we expect it to be harder)
- one with a shadow from your hand or phone across the page

**Name them like:** `01-neat.jpg`, `02-messy.jpg`, `03-ruled.jpg`, `04-holes.jpg`, `05-blue-pen.jpg`…
Put them straight into this folder (`draw-a-level/tests/real/`).
